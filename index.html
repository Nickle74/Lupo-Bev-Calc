Retailer
The core pricing calculator. Enter your bottle cost, size, and margin to see pour costs, cocktail pricing, and retail shelf prices for both on-premise (bars/restaurants) and off-premise (retail stores).
Distributor
Distributor-level pricing waterfall. Start with FOB, add taxes and fees to get the laid-in cost, then model discount tiers with weighted averages. Includes a full recap with net sales, GP dollars, and margin.
Proposal
Build screenshot-ready pricing proposals. Enter a brand, list price, and up to 7 deal levels. Generates a clean table with bottle cost and retail prices at 20-40% margins, ready to share with retailers or suppliers.
Price Chain
Reverse-engineer the supply chain. Start with the desired shelf retail price, set retailer and distributor margin targets, and see what the maximum laid-in cost needs to be to make the math work.
